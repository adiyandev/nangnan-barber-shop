THE IDEA

NangNan Barber Shop & Salon is built around a simple idea: getting your hair done should feel straightforward. You should be able to walk in with a reference, an idea or no idea at all and have a useful conversation about the look you want.

The shop brings barbering and salon-focused hair services together in Rayong, giving people a local place to refresh their look without making the experience complicated.

Because exact services, prices and availability can change, the website focuses on helping you understand the experience and contact the shop directly for the details that matter to your visit.
